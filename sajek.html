Sajek Valley

The Kingdom of Clouds

About Sajek Valley

Sajek Valley is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Bangladesh. It is located in Rangamati district and is famous for its beautiful hills, floating clouds, peaceful environment and breathtaking sunrise and sunset. Thousands of visitors come here every year to enjoy nature.


Travel Information

Estimated Budget

6000 - 9000 BDT per person


Duration

2 Days 1 Night


Best Time to Visit

October to March


Transportation

Take a bus from Dhaka to Khagrachari. From there, hire a Chander Gari (jeep) to reach Sajek Valley.

Travel Tips

  • Start your journey early in the morning.

  • Carry warm clothes during winter.

  • Book your resort before travelling.

  • Carry cash because ATM service is limited.

  • Respect the local tribal culture and keep the hills clean.
