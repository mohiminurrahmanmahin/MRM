Sundarbans

The Largest Mangrove Forest in the World

About Sundarbans

The Sundarbans is the largest mangrove forest in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is famous for the Royal Bengal Tiger, beautiful rivers, wildlife, and natural beauty. Nature lovers from all over the world visit this amazing forest every year.


Travel Information

Estimated Budget

8000 - 15000 BDT per person


Duration

3 Days 2 Nights


Best Time to Visit

November to February


Transportation

Travel by bus to Khulna or Mongla. Then take a tourist launch into the forest.

Travel Tips

  • Always travel with an authorized tour group.

  • Follow the instructions of the forest guide.

  • Carry mosquito repellent and necessary medicine.

  • Do not feed or disturb wild animals.

  • Keep the forest clean and avoid plastic waste.
