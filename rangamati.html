Rangamati

The Lake City of Bangladesh

About Rangamati

Rangamati is one of the most beautiful hill districts in Bangladesh. It is famous for Kaptai Lake, Hanging Bridge, tribal culture and green hills. It is an ideal destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.


Travel Information

Estimated Budget

5000 - 8000 BDT per person


Duration

2 Days 2 Nights


Best Time to Visit

October to March


Transportation

Travel by bus from Dhaka to Rangamati. Local boats and auto-rickshaws are available.

Travel Tips

  • Wear comfortable clothes and shoes.

  • Carry a camera for beautiful scenery.

  • Enjoy a boat ride on Kaptai Lake.

  • Respect local tribal culture and traditions.

  • Keep the hills and lake clean.
