Cox's Bazar

The Longest Natural Sea Beach in the World

About Cox's Bazar

Cox's Bazar is the most popular tourist destination in Bangladesh. It is famous for its long sandy beach, beautiful sunrise and sunset, Marine Drive Road, Himchari, Inani Beach and fresh seafood. Every year thousands of local and foreign tourists visit this beautiful place.

Travel Tips

  • Book hotel before travelling.
