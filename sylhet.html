Sylhet

Land of Tea Gardens, Waterfalls and Natural Beauty

About Sylhet

Sylhet is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Bangladesh. It is famous for Jaflong, Ratargul Swamp Forest, Bichanakandi, Lalakhal and endless tea gardens. Thousands of tourists visit Sylhet every year to enjoy its natural beauty.

Travel Tips

  • Carry an umbrella during the rainy season.

  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking.

  • Book hotels in advance during holidays.

  • Always bargain before hiring local transport.

  • Keep the tourist spots clean.
