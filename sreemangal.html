Sreemangal

The Tea Capital of Bangladesh

About Sreemangal

Sreemangal is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Bangladesh. It is famous for its endless tea gardens, Lawachara National Park, Madhabpur Lake and the unique seven-layer tea. Nature lovers visit Sreemangal throughout the year to enjoy its peaceful environment.


Travel Information

Estimated Budget

3500 - 6500 BDT per person


Duration

2 Days 1 Night


Best Time to Visit

October to March


Transportation

Bus and train services are available from Dhaka. Private cars can also be used.

Travel Tips

  • Visit tea gardens early in the morning.

  • Carry comfortable walking shoes.

  • Don't miss the famous seven-layer tea.

  • Carry a camera to capture nature.

  • Keep the tourist spots clean.
