Saint Martin Island

The Only Coral Island of Bangladesh

About Saint Martin

Saint Martin is the only coral island in Bangladesh. It is famous for its crystal-clear blue water, coral reefs, beautiful beach, coconut trees and fresh seafood. It is one of the most peaceful and attractive tourist destinations in the country.


Travel Information

Estimated Budget

7000 - 12000 BDT per person


Duration

3 Days 2 Nights


Best Time to Visit

November to March


Transportation

Travel by bus from Dhaka to Teknaf. Then take a ship to Saint Martin Island.

Travel Tips

  • Book your ship ticket before travelling.

  • Carry sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat.

  • Avoid touching or collecting coral.

  • Carry enough cash because ATM service is limited.

  • Keep the island clean and avoid plastic waste.
